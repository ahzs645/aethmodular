Where the Addis intercept comes from

We kept the IMPROVE calibration set and settings fixed and changed only the spectra. Raw spectra, with or without the region below 1425 cm⁻¹, give Addis a slope near 2 and an intercept near −5 µg/m³, close to SPARTAN's own calibration. Baseline-corrected spectra bring the intercept to about −1.2. That holds under our site-held-out protocol and under the Shiny app's.

What SPARTAN's calibration is

No paper or data file states SPARTAN's EC calibration settings in full. Four sources together get close, and the tests below check them against SPARTAN's published values for the 189 Addis filters.

AQRC "FTIR Calibration" Shiny app

global.R, R/selectorServer.R, R/calibrateServer.R (from Sean, July 2026)
  • Raw absorbance, every 4th point above 500 cm⁻¹ (2,722 points). No baseline correction.
  • 21 default IMPROVE sites, with an "All sites" box; analyst picks dates and filter lots.
  • Field blanks included by default, with EC set to 0; optional biomass-burning selection.
  • PLS (pls::plsr), up to 80 components, interleaved 10-fold CV.
  • The number of components is picked by clicking the error curve, so it is not recorded anywhere.

Anwar (2025), UC Davis dissertation

escholarship.org/uc/item/0nm8n2xr · chaired by Ann Dillner
  • EC calibration from "twenty-two diverse IMPROVE sites" against TOR EC; coefficients "matrix multiplied" onto SPARTAN spectra.
  • SPARTAN deposit area 3.53 cm², the same as IMPROVE, so no area correction.
  • Whole network (1,822 samples, 22 sites): FTIR EC vs HIPS −0.3 + 1.1x, R² 0.84.
  • Addis 2023 (Fig. S2.2): −3.9 + 1.8x, R² 0.75. The offset was already visible, treated as a quality check.
  • Christiansen-effect scattering at 2500–3500 cm⁻¹ in sulfate-rich samples, Addis included.

Debus et al. (2022), AMT 15, 2685, and its supplement

doi.org/10.5194/amt-15-2685-2022
  • IMPROVE's operational design: a Global model (all sites, every 5th sample), a Limited model (21 sites picked by clustering 2015 spectra; the app's default list), and a separate Biomass Burning model.
  • Calibrations use raw spectra (supplement S5).
  • No SPARTAN-specific settings.

SPARTAN papers and data release

Ren et al. (2025) Nat. Commun. 16, 7010 · Dillner et al. SPARTAN meeting slides (2023) · release v3.1.4
  • Only "calibrated to IMPROVE TOR" and "FTIR" (method 218); 25 mm Teflon, about 5 L/min.
  • Training years, components and spectral window are not given.

Your own app exports (24 June and 7 July 2026)

The coefficient files exported from the app on 24 June come from 916 IMPROVE filters, all on the biomass-burning list ("Only biomass burning"), May 2022 to April 2023. Refitting those exact spectra with our code reproduces both exported coefficient sets to within 6 × 10⁻¹², at k 31 and k 27. Applied to Addis they give 3.65x −11.73 and 3.43x −10.25 (r 0.963 against SPARTAN), so they are the smoke-only "smoke-906" model, not SPARTAN's.

Best reading: SPARTAN's EC comes from the app's recipe on raw spectra above 500 cm⁻¹, trained on a multi-year set of about 22 IMPROVE sites (or a broad network set), with roughly 17–20 components chosen by hand. Our reconstruction matches it to r 0.99. The leftover 0.3 µg/m³ is most likely training years we do not have (our spectra cover 2021–2023). Only AQRC's saved model file (the app's "Export Model" RDS, which stores the training filters and every k) would settle it.

Which wavenumbers push the Addis predictions

A PLS calibration is linear, so each Addis prediction splits exactly into one piece per wavenumber: coefficient × (Addis absorbance − IMPROVE mean absorbance) ÷ volume. What this means for the poster

  1. Step 1 mostly holds up. Split into steps (chart above): our calibration set changes almost nothing, leaving out 500–1425 cm⁻¹ moves the intercept about 1.3 µg/m³, baseline correction about 3.4, and the poster calibration's smaller component count about 0.4. Baseline correction is roughly 70% of the change.
  2. All three versions predict IMPROVE about equally well (test R² 0.73 to 0.76 at their chosen k). The difference only shows up on Addis, which fits the poster's point that IMPROVE-only checks hide the problem.
  3. SPARTAN's model behaves like a raw, full-range IMPROVE calibration with about 17–20 components, consistent with the app code, Debus et al. (2022) and Anwar (2025): ours matches its published EC filter by filter (r 0.99, RMSE 0.3 µg/m³). The exact training years and k need AQRC's saved model file.
  4. The raw models build Addis EC from large pieces that cancel: bands with no particle absorbers (3600–4000 and 1800–2800 cm⁻¹, only the sloping baseline) move it by 5 to 15 µg/m³ each. Addis's heavy, dusty filters have baselines unlike IMPROVE's, so the cancellation fails and the heavy-minus-light gap comes out about twice the HIPS gap. After baseline correction the model reads EC mostly through the C–H band, and Addis carries little organic carbon per unit EC, so it reads low.
  5. An earlier version of this page concluded the opposite (that leaving out 500–1425 cm⁻¹ did the work). That came from one corrupt IMPROVE spectrum (TONT1, 19 Nov 2022, sitting about 3 absorbance units high) that dominated the raw-spectra models. It is now excluded from every version.
Suggested Step 1 wording

Step 1. Baseline correction of the spectra (spline, 1425–4000 cm⁻¹). About 70% of the intercept change is the baseline correction itself; most of the rest comes from leaving out the region below 1425 cm⁻¹.

One clause on the poster keeps the before/after honest without changing the figure.

How this was run

  • Site-held-out (ours): IMPROVE lot 251, the poster's site split (about 9,000 training filters), site-grouped 5-fold CV, first major minimum, up to 30 components. Only the spectra change.
  • Shiny app: all 11,107 lot-251 filters, interleaved 10-fold CV, first k within 5% of the minimum, up to 80 components, model fitted on every filter. Its IMPROVE R² comes from the cross-validated predictions.
  • One IMPROVE filter with a corrupt raw spectrum (TONT1, 19 Nov 2022) is left out of every version and protocol. The spline result is unchanged by it (0.59x −1.21).
  • Spectra: the raw spectra the frozen spline run corrected (same bundle, content hash checked); “raw 1425–4000” is the spline window, “raw 4000–500” the full range the September explorer calibrations used.
  • Fits: Deming regression with the poster's error ratio (λ ≈ ), HIPS EC = Fabs / MAC 10. Spline at k = 8 reproduces the poster exactly (0.59x −1.21).
  • Contributions: app-style fit on every QC'd lot-251 filter; heaviest and lightest thirds are 63 filters each. Individual band values depend on how PLS spreads weight across correlated wavenumbers, so read the pattern and the totals rather than any single band.
  • Code: research/ftir_hips_chem/workflows/raw_vs_spline_20261020.py (app, contrib, spartan and freeze stages; the frozen model is in spartan_equivalent/); tables in output/tables/raw_vs_spline_20261020/.
