# The 1617 cm⁻¹ band — an Ethiopian marker, decoupled from the offset (2026-08-23)

> **Read the two CORRECTION sections at the end before quoting anything above
> them.** This doc is kept in narrative order because the reversals are the
> point. Short version: the title finding below ("the band tracks the offset")
> is **withdrawn**. Under a neutral baseline the 1617 band is present at
> **Addis and Bishoftu only** — and Bishoftu has the band with *no* offset, so
> the band does not carry the offset. It is a real, regional (Ethiopian)
> spectral marker. Three baselines gave three different answers, which is
> itself the methodological finding: APRLssb/AIRSpec anchors at 1520–1600 cm⁻¹,
> directly under the band.

## Original framing (superseded — see corrections)

Run after the Delhi/Beijing/Pasadena spectra targets became available, to answer
"can we compare Addis and Delhi spectra?" directly.

## Cross-site band table (median absorbance, all five targets)

| site | n | A@2920 (CH) | **A@1617** | A@3400 (OH) | A@4000 (background) | CH/bg | **1617/CH** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ETAD** Addis | 239 | 0.1880 | **0.0314** | 0.2371 | 0.2946 | 0.64 | **0.167** |
| **INDH** Delhi | 152 | 0.1718 | **0.0132** | 0.2079 | 0.2700 | 0.64 | **0.077** |
| CHTS Beijing | 192 | 0.1337 | −0.0018 | 0.1599 | 0.2176 | 0.61 | −0.014 |
| ETBI Bishoftu | 26 | 0.1185 | −0.0012 | 0.1488 | 0.1943 | 0.61 | −0.010 |
| USPA Pasadena | 158 | 0.0924 | −0.0094 | 0.1280 | 0.1743 | 0.53 | −0.102 |

A@4000 is a no-band window, so it reads pure baseline height.

## Finding 1 — the 1617 band splits the sites exactly as the offset does

The ~1617 cm⁻¹ feature (identified in ftir_12; established **not** to be amine)
is **strong at Addis, half-strength at Delhi, and absent at Beijing, Bishoftu
and Pasadena**. That is precisely the 2-vs-3 split of the Fabs-vs-EC offset
measured in `DUST_FE_TEST_2026-08-22.md`:

| site | 1617/CH | EC-only Fabs intercept |
|---|---|---|
| Addis | 0.167 | +28.4 Mm⁻¹ |
| Delhi | 0.077 | +35.3 |
| Beijing | −0.014 | +6.0 |
| Pasadena | −0.102 | +0.8 |
| Bishoftu | −0.010 | (no offset in transfer) |

## Finding 2 — within Addis, the band predicts absorption beyond loading

Per filter (n = 238): r(1617, Fabs) = **+0.704**, versus r(CH, Fabs) = +0.579
and r(background, Fabs) = +0.519. Controlling for CH (i.e. removing loading),
**partial r(1617, Fabs | CH) = +0.501**. So the band carries absorption
information that loading alone does not explain.

*Caveat:* the ratio form r(1617/CH, Fabs/CH) = −0.237. Ratio correlations with a
shared denominator are a known spurious-correlation trap, so the partial
correlation is the defensible statistic — but the disagreement means this should
be repeated with a proper multivariate fit before it is leaned on.

## Why this matters — it connects to "dark BrC"

~1600 cm⁻¹ aromatic C=C is the classic signature of **char / pyrolyzed biomass**.
Chakrabarty et al. (2023, *Nat. Geosci.* 16, 683) describe **"dark BrC"** with
AAE 1.5–2.0 contributing up to **half of absorption at 664 nm** — i.e. the one
non-EC absorber that *survives at HIPS's 633 nm*, where ordinary BrC has
collapsed to <10%. The site pattern fits: the band and the offset appear at the
two biomass/waste-burning-influenced megacities and are absent at coal/industrial
Beijing, traffic-dominated Pasadena, and small-town Bishoftu.

This is the **first spectral candidate for the offset's carrier**, and it was
explicitly flagged as the unaddressed hypothesis by the literature survey.

## Finding 3 — Addis's background is not anomalous vs Delhi

CH/background is 0.64 at *both* Addis and Delhi (Beijing/Bishoftu 0.61,
Pasadena 0.53). So the elevated Addis baseline is a **loading effect, not an
Addis peculiarity** — which predicts AIRSpec correction should help Delhi about
as much as it helps Addis. Directly testable now that the Delhi target exists.

## Caveats

Band heights are medians of an uncorrected (raw) absorbance at a single
wavenumber; the 1617 assignment remains unresolved between aromatic C=C,
carboxylate COO⁻, and water bending (~1640). Negative values at the low-loading
sites indicate baseline curvature at that point, not negative absorption — the
comparison should be repeated on AIRSpec-corrected spectra (available for every
target). Delhi's Fabs intercept came from n = 27 in the Fe regression (n = 61 in
the curvature fit). Correlation is not attribution.

# CORRECTION (same day) — the raw-spectra pattern was largely a baseline artifact

Step 1 of "Next" was run immediately (repeat on AIRSpec-corrected spectra) and it
**partially retracts Finding 1 above**. Figure:
`output/plots/cross_site_spectra_2026-08-23.png`.

## On corrected spectra the 2-vs-3 split disappears

| site | 1617/CH **raw** | 1617/CH **corrected** | genuine local peak in 1560–1680? | peak position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ETAD Addis | 0.167 | 0.332 | **yes** | **1620 cm⁻¹** |
| INDH Delhi | 0.077 | 0.328 | no (window edge) | 1678 |
| CHTS Beijing | −0.014 | 0.288 | no (window edge) | 1679 |
| ETBI Bishoftu | −0.010 | 0.135 | no (window edge) | 1679 |
| USPA Pasadena | −0.102 | 0.418 | yes (weak) | 1635 |

The raw-spectra ordering (Addis ≫ Delhi ≫ others) does **not** survive baseline
correction: on corrected spectra Addis (0.332) and Delhi (0.328) are
indistinguishable and Pasadena is highest (0.418). The apparent raw split was
mostly the sloping PTFE background, which is steepest at the most-loaded sites.

## What does survive — and it is still interesting

Peak-shape analysis of the corrected median spectra (height above the 1480–1560
/ 1680–1760 shoulders):

- **Addis alone has a genuine interior local maximum at ~1620 cm⁻¹** (+0.0035).
- Delhi and Beijing peak at the *window edge* (1678–1679): their 1500–1800
  region is dominated by a large carbonyl band near 1700–1720, with no separate
  1620 feature — visible directly in panel C of the figure.
- Pasadena has a weak, distinct maximum at 1635 (+0.0024), likelier water
  bending than aromatic C=C.
- Bishoftu shows no interior peak.

**So the 1620 band is Addis-specific, but it does NOT track the offset** —
Delhi has the largest offset (+35.3 Mm⁻¹) and no 1620 band. The
"band explains the offset" hypothesis in Finding 1 is therefore **not
supported**; the band remains a genuine, unexplained Addis marker (consistent
with ftir_12), and the dark-BrC idea now needs a different line of evidence.

Finding 2 (within-Addis partial correlation, +0.50) was computed on raw spectra
and inherits the same baseline confound — it must be recomputed on corrected
spectra before it means anything.

Finding 3 (Addis background not anomalous vs Delhi) is unaffected and is
visible in panel A: raw backgrounds order purely by loading.

## Bonus observation from the figure

Panel B shows **Bishoftu with the highest corrected O–H/C–H region absorbance of
all five sites and Addis the lowest** — the "Addis is a deficit" result
(ftir_17) rendered directly, and now with an in-country comparison that makes it
much harder to attribute to network or instrument differences.

# CORRECTION 2 — the AIRSpec anchor sits *underneath* the band

A literature check flagged that APRLssb anchors Segment 2 at the **minimum
absorbance in [1520, 1600] cm⁻¹**. Verified in our own port
(`scripts/airspec_baseline.py`): `find_min_pos(..., interval=(1600.0, 1520.0))`,
and `_fit_spline` gives that window **zero weight**. That anchor sits directly on
the low-wavenumber shoulder of a 1617 band, so **AIRSpec correction is
structurally disposed to suppress it**. Correction 1 above therefore used the
wrong instrument to judge the band, exactly as raw spectra used the wrong one in
the other direction (sloping PTFE background).

Neither raw nor AIRSpec-corrected spectra can settle this. So: a **third,
neutral baseline** — `pybaselines.spline.pspline_arpls` (lam 1e6), no anchor
window, saturated PTFE (1300–1100 cm⁻¹) and <700 cm⁻¹ masked.

## Result with a neutral baseline

| site | n | A@1617 | 1617/CH | **genuine local peak in 1560–1680?** | peak position |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ETAD** Addis | 239 | 0.0139 | 0.95 | **yes** | **1617** |
| **ETBI** Bishoftu | 26 | 0.0219 | 1.03 | **yes** | **1617** |
| INDH Delhi | 152 | 0.0172 | 0.75 | no | 1679 (carbonyl edge) |
| CHTS Beijing | 192 | 0.0156 | 0.71 | no | 1679 (carbonyl edge) |
| USPA Pasadena | 158 | 0.0170 | 2.70 | yes | 1634 (likely water bend) |

## The disentangled conclusion

**Both Ethiopian sites — and only the Ethiopian sites — show a genuine local
maximum at exactly 1617 cm⁻¹.** Bishoftu's is if anything stronger relative to
CH (1.03 vs 0.95) than Addis's. Delhi and Beijing have no 1617 feature at all;
their 1500–1800 region is dominated by a carbonyl band at ~1700–1720. Pasadena's
1634 peak is a different feature, most likely water bending.

This separates two things that were being conflated:

| | sites | most likely nature |
|---|---|---|
| **1617 cm⁻¹ band** | Addis **+ Bishoftu** (Ethiopia) | regional **fuel chemistry** — charcoal/eucalyptus biomass burning, consistent with Tefera et al. 2021 (46% of Addis OC from biomass burning) |
| **Fabs-vs-EC offset** | Addis **+ Delhi** (high loading) | **loading-dependent**, instrumental — Bishoftu has the band but no offset |

**Bishoftu is the decisive case: strongest 1617 band of the five, no absorption
offset.** So the band does not carry the offset — Correction 1's conclusion
survives, but for a better reason and with the band's status upgraded rather
than demoted. It is a real, regional, previously uncharacterized spectral
feature.

## Methodological lesson worth keeping

Three baselines gave three different answers to "is there a 1617 band":
raw said Addis ≫ Delhi ≫ none; AIRSpec said no site-specific band; neutral
pspline_arpls says Ethiopia-only. **Any band claim in 1500–1650 cm⁻¹ from this
lineage must state its baseline and show robustness across at least two.** The
APRL/AIRSpec anchor makes it structurally unable to see bands in that window —
which also means the *published* aerosol-FTIR literature built on that toolchain
would not have reported such a band even if present.
