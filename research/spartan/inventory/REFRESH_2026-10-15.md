`--check-updates` pass before it reported 73 of 233 files with changed content
(ChemSpecPM10, ChemSpecPM25 and ReconstrPM25 at most sites); no files were
added or removed. A second check after the pull reported zero changes.

The 2026-09-22 cache was kept outside the repo, so ChemSpecPM25 was compared
filter by filter (mean of repeat reports per filter and parameter, the
convention the gallery exports use):

- **88 new filters**: INDH 34, INJA 21, KRUL 16, AUMN 11, IDBD 6.
- **2 filters removed**: one each at ILHA and INDH.
- **47 revised values**, all ions: sulfate 22, nitrate 22, nitrite 3, at
  AUMN, ETBI, MXMC, CASH and AEAZ. Comparing raw rows instead reports
  ~9,900 "changes"; those are repeat reports of one parameter matched in a
  different order, not revisions.
- **ILHA-0216 is dated 2026-11-17**, after the pull, almost certainly a date
  typo in the public file. `gallery/data/export_spartan_reference.py` and
  `export_spartan_map.py` leave out filters dated after the export day and
  print them.

Gallery exports re-run on this pull: `spartan_reference.json` (now every
ChemSpecPM25 parameter, plus HIPS batches 1–51 and FTIR functional groups from
the lab batch 2–3 deliverable), `spartan_map.json`, and `weather/filters.json`
/ `roses.json`. `filters.json` (the four focus sites) reads the unified filter
dataset, not this mirror, and was not rebuilt.
