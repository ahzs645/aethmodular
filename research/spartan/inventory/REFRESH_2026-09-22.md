live `uv run aeth spartan pull --check-updates` reported zero additions,
removals, or content-hash changes after the refresh.

## Carbon provenance in refreshed ChemSpecPM25

For CHTS, INDH, USPA, and ETAD, public `EC PM2.5` and `OC PM2.5` rows carry
`Analysis_Description = FTIR`. Public `BC PM2.5` rows carry HIPS or an optical
estimate; Beijing includes method 220, “Estimated from the HIPS-SSR BC curve.”
These fields do not add independent TOR measurements to the four-site gallery.
The public CSV refresh does not rebuild the gallery’s frozen filter export or
the older HIPS/IMPROVE bridge analyses.
