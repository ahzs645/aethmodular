How the field does this — literature quick reference (full survey: research/ftir_ec_phase3/SPECTRAL_SIMILARITY_LITERATURE.md)

LOCAL (Shenk & Westerhaus 1997) — the NIR standard: rank the library by Pearson correlation on derivative spectra against each target, calibrate on the top-k. Our Pearson / 2nd-deriv combination reproduces its selection step.

LWR (Næs 1990) — nearest neighbours by distance in PC score space, distance-weighted fit. Reggente, Dillner & Takahama (AMT 2016) used Mahalanobis distance in score space as the extrapolation diagnostic for these exact IMPROVE calibrations — our Mahalanobis-PCA metric turns that diagnostic into a selector.

SAM / cosine (remote sensing; Kruse 1993) — angle between spectra; equals Pearson after mean-centering.

RS-LOCAL (Lobsey 2017, soils) — resample library subsets, keep samples that recur in subsets that predict the target well. The only method optimizing the real objective; a candidate batch-mode extension here.

Weakley (2016/18) — CSN sites with atypical composition (Elizabeth NJ) needed their own calibration: the aerosol-FTIR precedent that subsetting fixes what pooled models break.

Kennard–Stone / DUPLEX / SPXY are spanning designs (coverage, not target-matching) — useful only for cal/val splits of a selected subset. Nobody in aerosol FTIR has published per-target-site library selection; doing it here is novel but in-tradition.
